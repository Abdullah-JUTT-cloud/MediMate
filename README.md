# MedAlerto

## Startup Overview

MedAlerto is a modern clinic management platform built for doctors who want to spend less time on paperwork and more time with patients. It helps clinics digitize the full consultation workflow, from patient records and checkups to prescriptions, appointments, and follow-up communication.

## The Problem

Many clinics still rely on manual processes that create daily friction:

- Paper prescriptions are slow to produce, easy to lose, and hard to track.
- Patient records are scattered across notes, files, and chat history.
- Appointment handling becomes chaotic during reschedules or emergencies.
- Follow-ups are inconsistent, which affects patient experience.

## Our Solution

MedAlerto brings core clinic operations into one connected system:

- Doctor authentication with secure access control.
- Patient management with centralized records.
- Checkup and diagnosis tracking for each visit.
- Professional PDF prescriptions, including medicines, lab tests, and next appointment details.
- Instant prescription delivery through WhatsApp.
- Smart appointment workflows, including emergency cancellation messaging.
- Practice insights to track clinic performance.

## Who It Is For

- Private clinic doctors
- Small to medium healthcare practices
- Teams that need a practical, easy-to-adopt digital workflow

## Why MedAlerto

- Saves clinical admin time
- Improves prescription quality and consistency
- Reduces communication gaps with patients
- Improves operational visibility for growth

## One-Line Pitch

MedAlerto is a smart clinic operating platform that helps doctors manage patients, appointments, prescriptions, and follow-ups in one place.
